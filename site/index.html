The problem

Agents are infinitely patient and infinitely agreeable, so they help you build forever — and ship never.

What worked

The magic at Habitat nights was never the advice. It was the container: a clock, a locked scope, and a room waiting to see what you made.

The move

We put the container inside Cursor and Claude Code. Same ritual: lock, build, ship, roast.

The ship wall is public and starts empty on purpose. Run a sprint, open a PR with your log. be the first hop →
