# Lock gates

The idea may only be locked when all four pass.

1. **Problem.** Names a specific person, a specific moment, a specific pain.
   - Weak: "people want healthier food."
   - Strong: "health-conscious professionals cannot find takeout that fits their allergies in under 5 minutes on a lunch break."
2. **ICP.** Specific enough that the builder knows where to find 10 of them this week. "Everyone", "millennials", "students", "developers", "founders", "businesses" alone all fail.
3. **One-liner.** One sentence a non-technical friend can repeat. It names the ONE feature that proves the value. Not a platform. Not a feature list. Hard fail over 220 characters.
4. **Out of scope.** At least two things the builder is explicitly NOT building tonight. Auth, settings, admin panels, and payments are the usual suspects.

Event gate (only in event mode): the idea must fit the theme and be submittable under the rules in `.habitat/event.md`.
