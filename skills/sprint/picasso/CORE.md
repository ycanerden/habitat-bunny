# Core

The core is the one thing the artifact exists to carry. A sentence has one fact. A plan has one deliverable. A product has one one-liner. Everything else is shading.

A cut is honest when:

- Each state is shorter than the one before, and something real left between them
- The last state still does the one thing without being told what was removed
- No remaining word, step, or feature survives the test: take it out, meaning changes
- The builder can say the one-liner without "and also"

A cut is fake when:

- A word was swapped for a shorter word and nothing else moved
- The extra feature is "just hidden in a menu"
- The plan still has a step that could happen after the URL exists
- The pitch starts with the company and arrives at the problem in paragraph four
- "Coming soon" stands where a feature used to be

## Le Taureau

Eleven lithographs, December 1945 to January 1946. State one is a bull with weight, hide, and shadow. State eleven is a handful of lines. It is still the bull. Nothing was added along the way.

The rule Habitat took from it: shade last, and usually never. Delete, check, delete.
