# Core

The core is one sentence. Everything else is shading.

A cut is honest when:

- A stranger can use the artifact without being told what was removed
- The builder can say the one-liner without listing "and also"
- Copy has no word that would survive deletion without a change in meaning
- Customer talk has one ask, not a tour

A cut is fake when:

- The extra feature is "just hidden in a menu"
- The landing page still names three products
- The pitch starts with the company and arrives at the problem in paragraph four
- You replaced a long adjective with a different long adjective
