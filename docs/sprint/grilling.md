## What it does

`grilling` is the idea hop. One question at a time, no compliments, until four gates pass: a specific problem, a findable ICP, a one-liner a friend can repeat, and two things you are not building tonight.

The defining constraint: the grill does not end when you are tired of questions. It ends when the gates pass. A vague ICP is a failed lock, not a vibe.

## When to reach for it

Type `/grilling`, or the agent reaches for it automatically when you are about to build without a one-liner, or when you ask to lock / sharpen / stress-test tonight's idea.

Reach for this when the idea is mush. If the idea is already locked, you want [check-scope](./check-scope.md) for new features, not a second grill.

## One question, then wait

This is not a questionnaire. It is a conversation that refuses to slide into agreeable-assistant mode. If an answer is vague, it re-asks. It does not summarize you back to you as filler.

## Common questions

**Why won't it let me start coding?**
Because the number one reason people do not ship is building the wrong surface. The lock is cheaper than the rewrite.

**What counts as an ICP?**
Someone you could find ten of this week. "Founders" is a crowd. "First-time solo founders who already have a landing page and no users" is an ICP.

**Can I skip to build if I already know?**
Then the gates pass in three questions and you lock. Knowing is fine. Claiming you know while saying "users" is not.

## It's working if

- You were asked a question you tried to dodge.
- The one-liner fits in one breath.
- Two tempting features are written down as not tonight.
