## What it does

`today` is the on-ramp for a builder who is already in the mess. It locks one sentence for today and refuses to stop before that sentence has a URL.

The defining constraint: you do not need to say a ritual phrase. A ramble ("I am building something and I am not sure") is enough input.

## When to reach for it

You invoke this by typing `/today`, and the agent won't reach for it on its own.

Reach for it when you are mid-build and lost. If you have a free evening and want the full ritual (team, event, roast as ceremony), use [start-sprint](../sprint/start-sprint.md).

## One hop, not a smaller essay

Today is smaller than a sprint, not sloppier. The four lock gates still apply. "Work on the app" still fails.

## Common questions

**Is this the same as a 4-hour sprint?**
Same gates, thinner container. No onboarding about team vs event unless you bring it up. The point is to unstick you, not to run a night.

**What if I already have a lock from last night?**
Do not re-litigate it. New ideas go through [check-scope](../sprint/check-scope.md).

## It's working if

- You can say today's sentence without listing a platform.
- You have a public URL or a parked idea, not a longer todo list.

## Where it fits

Standalone on-ramp that still uses grilling, check-scope, [picasso](../sprint/picasso.md), ship, roast underneath. Map: [ask-bunny](../sprint/ask-bunny.md).
